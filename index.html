How it works

  1. Step 1
    Ingest

    New federal opportunities are pulled from grants.gov daily, with full synopsis and eligibility.

  2. Step 2
    Profile

    Each faculty member's research profile is built from their publications (OpenAlex / ORCID).

  3. Step 3
    Match

    Semantic retrieval finds candidate opportunities; an LLM reranks them and writes the rationale.

  4. Step 4
    Agents

    Specialised agents suggest collaborators, propose proposal angles, and flag eligibility risks.

  5. Step 5
    Deliver

    A weekly email plus this portal, where faculty give feedback that tunes future matches.
